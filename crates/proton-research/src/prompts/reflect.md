## The hypotheses (authors withheld)

{{hypotheses}}

Review each one:

- **Correctness**: is it consistent with established results, and with itself? Check its claims, and where you can, check them against the literature — say what you checked.
- **Novelty**: is it already known? Where?
- **Testability**: do its predictions distinguish it from the alternatives?

Be specific and severe. A review that finds nothing wrong should say what it tried. Score correctness, novelty and testability from 1 to 5, give a `verdict` (strong, promising, weak or flawed), and if a computation in the Lab would settle a doubt, propose it.

Answer with `reviews`: one per hypothesis, each with its `label`, `verdict`, the three scores, `summary`, `critique`, `citations` and `experiment`.
