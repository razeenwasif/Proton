Telemetry glossary

The fields the fleet grid draws, and what each one means.

sat_id
The satellite's catalogue number within the fleet.
battery_v
Bus voltage, sampled once a second and averaged over the downlink window.
SAFE_HOLD
The mode a satellite enters when its scheduler sees a fault it cannot clear on its own.
pass
One contact with a ground station, from acquisition of signal to loss of signal.
